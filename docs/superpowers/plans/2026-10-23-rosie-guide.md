# Rosie Guide and Simplified Trainer Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add Rosie, a cute anime-style guide adapted from the Cal Poly Rose Float logo, who welcomes a first-time learner and leads every lesson step through look → watch → try → result, while the home screen and lesson panel show one thing and one next action at a time.

**Architecture:** Pure content and logic modules (`content/mascot.mjs`, `logic/step-beats.mjs`) carry the words and the beat table and are unit-tested; the renderer controller keeps `state.guide` and `state.lesson.beat` and the existing reducer; a shared `ui/guide.mjs` card (avatar + speech bubble) is reused by the welcome dialog, Home, the level map, and the lesson panel. Five SVG expressions are generated by a script and shipped as files under `src/renderer/assets/mascot/`.

**Tech Stack:** Electron 44.6.0, Electron Forge 8.0.1, dependency-free ES modules, `node --test`, the hidden-window smoke harness.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-rosie-guide-design.md` (argues from `one-file-promot-float-lab/ONE_FILE_TASK.md`, revision 1).

## Global Constraints

- Product name stays “Roseflow PC Mac Training”; independent, offline, simulation-only; no network, remote asset, or hardware command.
- Level names exactly `Basic`, `intermediate`, `I hate my life`, available on Home.
- Lesson ids, step ids, teaching text, questions, answer keys, hints, validators, the recreated windows, help, and handbook are not changed.
- Progress schema version stays 3; the new `guide.welcomed` field is optional and defaults to `true` when absent; restart still needs confirmation.
- Electron isolation, sandbox, CSP (`style-src 'self'`: no inline `style=` attributes in markup or SVG), validated IPC, navigation block, and link allowlist unchanged.
- Nothing is staged, committed, or pushed; no plan step commits.
- Automated checks run in the hidden window only; never show a window, take focus, or touch the keyboard or clipboard.
- Files under `one-file-promot-float-lab/` are not modified.

## Review Focus

1. A fresh launch whose progress file cannot be saved (the `savefail` scenario): the welcome must still open and close without an error, and the save-failure banner must still appear. Pinned in Task 7 (`scenarioSaveFail`).
2. A schema-3 file written before this build (no `guide` key) or a migrated v1/v2 file: no welcome dialog, Rosie greets on Home. Pinned in Task 1 (schema test) and Task 7 (`scenarioLegacy2`).
3. A learner who drags in the Edit View during the look beat: the check still runs and the panel jumps to the try beat with the feedback. Pinned in Task 7 (`scenarioFirst`).
4. Back pressed while a demonstration runs: the demo is cancelled, show data restored, and the previous step opens at its first beat. Pinned in Task 7 (`scenarioFirst`).
5. The minimum window (1024 × 680): the welcome dialog and the lesson guide card fit without clipping the Back / Next row. Pinned in Task 7 (`scenarioShots`, a 1024 × 680 capture reviewed by eye).
